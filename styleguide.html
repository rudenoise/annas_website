Brand & Style Guide

The Hughes Practice

A single reference for how the website looks, sounds and behaves — so every future change stays consistent. Colours, type and components below mirror the live site exactly.

01 — Personality

Brand personality

Warm, calm and human, with quiet confidence. The practice should feel like a safe, unhurried space — never clinical, never salesy. Modern and considered, but soft.

Calm Warm Trustworthy Unhurried Human Approachable
02 — Logo

Logo

The wordmark reads THE HUGHES PRACTICE with the subtitle THERAPY FOR REAL LIFE. Both are set in Roboto, with custom letter-spacing and an outline effect applied in Canva (baked into the artwork).

Usage

✓ Do
  • Use the black wordmark on light backgrounds (BRISK / white).
  • On dark backgrounds, use an off-white version of the wordmark.
  • Give it clear space; keep it around 50px tall in the header.
✕ Don't
  • Place the black logo on a dark or busy background.
  • Stretch, recolour arbitrarily, or add effects.
  • Re-type the name in another font instead of using the artwork.

04 — Typography

Typography

One typeface throughout: Roboto Mono (Google Fonts). The monospace character gives the brand its distinctive, considered feel. Body text is ExtraLight (200); heavier weights are used only for emphasis and small labels.

05 — Buttons

Buttons

All buttons are fully-rounded pills. Each has a defined role — don't mix their colours or purposes.

Arrange a free call Book a free call → Send message Secondary
  • Hero CTA (EROS pink) Main page call-to-action · uppercase, letter-spaced, light weight
  • Nav button (Coral) “Book a free call” in the header only · white text
  • Light (Apricot) Form submit (“Send message”) · dark text
  • Ghost (Green outline) Low-emphasis secondary actions
06 — Iconography & imagery

Iconography & imagery

Simple single-line icons, drawn in black at an even, medium stroke weight (~2px). Keep all icons in one visual family and one weight.

Benefit icons: 56px circle, icon in black, circle colour rotates EROS → LIDO → APRICOT.

Photography & illustration

Real, natural portraiture (soft, warm, in-context). Decorative illustration is single-line and minimal — like the hero flower. Both stay understated so the words lead.

08 — Layout & spacing

Layout & spacing

  • Content max width 1140px, centred, 28px side padding
  • Section rhythm 90px top/bottom (64px on mobile)
  • Alternating bands off-white ↔ white, with colour bands for key moments
  • Corner radius Cards 18px · pills/buttons full (999px)
  • Mobile breakpoint 1000px — nav collapses to a menu button
  • Borders 1px hairline in “Line” (#E4DED3)
09 — Voice & tone

Voice & tone

Speak as Anna — first person, warm and direct. Plain, gentle language. Reassure without pressure. British English spelling.

✓ Do
  • “There's no rush and no obligation.”
  • Short sentences; everyday words.
  • Acknowledge how hard reaching out can feel.
  • Be specific: Bristol, online, £50, 15-minute call.
✕ Don't
  • Clinical jargon or diagnostic labels.
  • Hype, urgency, or hard-sell language.
  • Over-promising “fixes” or guaranteed outcomes.
  • Long, dense paragraphs.
