I'm Anna.

I'm a qualified therapist offering a warm, supportive, and non-judgemental space where you can talk openly about what's going on for you.

I mostly work in person from a beautiful space in Bristol. I also offer online sessions.

I hold a Postgraduate Level 4 Diploma in Therapeutic Counselling, my training is in the person-centred approach, and I'm a registered member of the BACP, which means I work to a professional, ethical framework you can trust.

Reaching out can feel like a big step. There's no rush and no obligation — a free introductory call is simply a chance to talk, get a feel for how it might be to work together, and decide what's right for you.

Rather than quick fixes, our work together will give you space to explore things properly, make sense of your experiences, and move forward with greater clarity and confidence.

If you're curious about how therapy could help, I invite you to book a free introductory call.

Many people find that when they feel genuinely heard and understood, things begin to shift.

Therapy can help you make sense of difficult experiences, strengthen your relationship with yourself, and move forward in a way that feels authentic and meaningful.

The practical details.

Where

In person in Bristol, or online by video anywhere in the UK — whichever feels more comfortable for you.

Sessions

Sessions last 50 minutes and usually take place weekly, at a regular time that suits you.

Fees

£50 per session. A limited number of reduced-rate spaces are available — please do ask.
