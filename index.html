Hi, I'm Anna

I'm a qualified therapist offering therapy for real life.

This idea comes off the back of hearing many people — clients, friends, acquaintances — say that their problems aren't big enough for therapy.

But life throws different challenges at us all. And if something is causing you pain and/or unease, then I believe it's worth talking about.

There's no hierarchy of pain.

Maybe you're carrying years of trauma or the pain of bereavement. Maybe you're suffering with crippling anxiety.

Or perhaps you're stuck in the same relationship patterns, going through a breakup, struggling with family dynamics or you're feeling overwhelmed at work.

Whatever brings you here, my aim is to offer a comfortable, safe space where you can feel heard, understood, and accepted.

Therapy can be an incredibly thought-provoking, healing process that helps you make sense of your experiences, understand the patterns that shape your life, and create meaningful change.

If you're curious about how therapy could help, I invite you to book a free, 20-minute introductory call.

This is simply a time to talk, get a feel for how it might be to work together, and decide what's right for you. No pressure to commit, just a chance to test the water.

If after that you want to book a session, we can meet face to face from a beautiful space in Bristol, or online if getting there is tricky.

Frequently asked questions.

Where do sessions take place?
I offer sessions in person from a comfortable private space in Bristol (exact location tbc). I also offer online sessions. In addition, I offer 'Walk and Talk' sessions, which take place out in nature. Find out more about Walk and Talk sessions here.
How much does a session cost?
After the free 20 minute introductory call, our first session would be half price at £25. This is because we would spend some time going over how the therapy works and the details of our contract together, a crucial stage for ensuring a safe and ethical approach to the work as per the BACP Ethical Framework. After this, there is still time left to talk more about you and what has brought you to therapy. After this, all sessions are £50 and last 50-55 minutes.
What happens in the free introductory call?
It's a relaxed, no-pressure phone call of around 20 minutes. It's a chance to tell me a little about what's bringing you to therapy, ask any questions, and get a feel for how it might be to work together. There's no obligation to book afterwards.
How often would we meet, and for how long?
Sessions usually take place weekly, at a regular time. Rather than offering quick fixes, I work at your pace — some people come for a few months, others for longer. We'll review how things are going together, and you're always free to decide what's right for you.
Are you qualified and registered?
Yes. I hold a CPCAB Level 4 Diploma in Therapeutic Counselling, alongside ongoing professional development and regular clinical supervision. I'm a BACP Individual Member — The British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (BACP) is one of the UK's largest and most respected professional bodies for counsellors and psychotherapists. This means I work within a recognised professional and ethical framework.
What type of therapy do you offer?
I work using a person-centred approach which means I believe that you are the expert on your own life and experiences. My role is to empower you to discover the answers that are right for you. What may work for me may not be right for you, so rather than giving advice or telling you what to do, I can offer information, support, and a safe space to explore your thoughts and feelings.
What can therapy help with?
People come to me with a wide range of experiences — including anxiety and worry, low mood and depression, stress and burnout, relationship difficulties, grief and loss, life changes and transitions, low self-worth, and simply feeling stuck. You don't need a specific reason to come to therapy.
Is what I share confidential?
Yes. What you share with me is treated as confidential, and I'll explain how confidentiality works — including its rare limits — at the start of our work together. Any details you send through the contact form are only used to respond to your enquiry, as set out in the privacy policy.
