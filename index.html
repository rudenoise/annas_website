A different kind of conversation

You're the expert on your own life.

My work starts from a simple idea: my role isn't to diagnose you, fix you, or tell you what to do. It's to offer a warm, genuine and completely non-judgemental space where you can think out loud, feel whatever you're feeling, and work things through in your own time.

Some people come to counselling in the middle of a crisis. Others just sense that something isn't quite right, or want to understand themselves a little better. Both are completely valid reasons to be here. You don't need to have the words ready or know where to start — we'll find that together.

How I work

Three things you can count on.

Led by you

There's no fixed agenda and no script. We follow what matters to you — you set the direction and the pace, every session.

Without judgement

Whatever you bring, you'll be met with warmth and acceptance. Not advice, not analysis, and never being told you're wrong to feel how you feel.

At your own pace

Some sessions go deep; others are quieter. There's no pressure to perform, and no deadline to be "better" by.

Hello

I'm Anna.

I'm a counsellor based in Bristol, supporting adults through periods of change, uncertainty and self-discovery — in person and online across the UK. My training is in the person-centred approach, and I'm a registered member of the BACP, which means I work to a professional, ethical framework you can trust.

I know that reaching out can feel like a big step. There's no rush and no obligation — a free introductory call is simply a chance to talk, get a feel for how it might be to work together, and decide what's right for you.
